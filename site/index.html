Guide

Set It Up In 4 Steps

Your phone or laptop must be on the same Wi‑Fi network as the computer running OBS.

1

Install the plugin

  1. Close OBS, then double-click vcast-…-windows-x64-Installer.exe.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway. The installer isn't code-signed yet.
  3. Click Install. It also allows OBS through Windows Firewall so phones can reach it.
  1. Close OBS, then double-click vcast-…-macos-universal.pkg.
  2. If macOS blocks it, right-click the .pkg → Open → Open. You can also use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. When OBS starts, allow Local Network access and incoming connections if asked.
  1. Run sudo apt install ./vcast-*-x86_64-linux-gnu.deb, then start OBS.
2

Add the screen receiver

In OBS, open Docks → Vcast and click Add screen receiver to scene. You can also add it from Sources → + → Vcast Screen Receiver. It joins your live scene and fills the screen.

3

Cast from your device

  1. Open Control Center (on Mac, the Control Center icon in the menu bar).
  2. Tap Screen Mirroring.
  3. Choose Vcast (your computer's name). Screen and sound appear in OBS.
  1. Install the Vcast app from the download above, or open https://<your-PC>:8443/android on the phone. Allow installing apps from your browser when asked.
  2. Open Vcast, tap your OBS computer, then Cast screen. If Android offers a choice, pick Entire screen (not a single app), then tap Start now. Tick Share phone audio first for sound (Android 10+).
  3. Add the Cast to OBS tile to Quick Settings (swipe down twice → pencil icon → drag the tile) for one-tap casting.

Android's built-in "Cast" button only works with Google-certified devices, so OBS can't appear there. The Vcast app and its tile take its place.

  1. Find the cast page address in the Vcast dock, e.g. https://192.168.1.20:8443.
  2. Open it in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. The first time, click Advanced → Proceed on the certificate warning; the certificate is private to your computer.
  3. Press Share screen, choose a screen or window, and tick share audio for sound.

Windows laptop, OBS on Windows: just press Win + K and pick the OBS computer. Add the Vcast Win+K Receiver first (Vcast dock → Add Win+K receiver to scene).

4

Go live

The cast appears in your Program output. Stream, record or project it like any other source. To show two phones side by side, add a second receiver with a different name.

Help

Troubleshooting & FAQ

My iPhone doesn't show "Vcast" in Screen Mirroring
  • Make sure the iPhone and the OBS computer are on the same Wi‑Fi, with no VPN on either.
  • Guest networks and "AP/client isolation" on some routers hide devices from each other.
  • The Vcast Screen Receiver source must exist with Accept AirPlay ticked.
  • Windows: allow OBS through the firewall. Mac: allow OBS under Privacy & Security → Local Network.
Why isn't OBS in my Android phone's Cast menu?

Android's Cast menu (Google Cast / Chromecast) only lists devices with a security certificate issued by Google, which no PC software can get. The free Vcast app and its Cast to OBS tile give you the same one-tap experience. If OBS runs on Windows, Android's Cast / Smart View (Miracast) and a laptop's Win + K also work: add the Vcast Win+K Receiver source.

Which phones work?
  • iPhone and iPad: any model with Screen Mirroring in Control Center. Nothing to install on the phone.
  • Mac: Screen Mirroring in the menu bar, no app needed.
  • Android: Android 8.0 or newer with the free Vcast app. Phone sound needs Android 10 or newer.
The Android app doesn't find my computer
  • Check that OBS is open and has a Vcast Screen Receiver with Accept the Vcast Android app ticked.
  • Phone and computer must be on the same Wi‑Fi (not a guest network), with no VPN.
  • Tap Connect by address and type the IP shown in the Vcast dock, e.g. 192.168.1.20.
  • Windows: set the Wi‑Fi to Private. Firewalls must allow UDP 48689 and TCP 48690.
Some apps show a black screen or no sound

Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, banking apps and similar protected apps block screen recording, on iPhone and Android alike, so they show black in OBS. On Android, a few apps also refuse to share their sound. This is the app's choice and no casting tool can get around it. YouTube, games, photos, browsers and most apps work.

How do I stop casting?

iPhone/iPad: Control Center → Screen Mirroring → Stop Mirroring. Android: tap Stop in the app or in its notification, or tap the Cast to OBS tile again. Laptop: press Stop sharing. Tick Keep showing the last picture in the receiver's properties if you don't want OBS to go blank.

The picture is choppy or delayed

Move closer to the router or use 5 GHz Wi‑Fi. Choose 720p in the Android app or on the laptop cast page. A short delay (well under a second) is normal over Wi‑Fi.

OBS says the plugin failed to load

Update OBS to 32.2 or newer (Help → About). Then check Help → Log Files → View Current Log for lines starting with [vcast].

Is it free? Is it safe?

Yes. Vcast is free, open source software. Everything stays on your local network.
